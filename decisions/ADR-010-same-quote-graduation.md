# ADR-010: Same-Quote Graduation

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Scope:** RetroPick Launchpad V2

## Decision proposed

The quote asset used during primary bonding should remain the quote asset of the mature destination market by default.

## Rationale

Avoid hidden conversion, oracle dependence, extra slippage/MEV and ambiguous graduation accounting.

Cross-quote graduation requires a separate explicit design and qualification.
