## ADR-021: terminal quote is a path-dependent admission input

The [one-shot witness](../../../evidence/launchpad/kuru/prelaunch-q-threshold-counterexample-2026-09-30.md) and the [round-trip witness](../../../evidence/launchpad/kuru/prelaunch-round-trip-witness-2026-09-30.md) use the same valid launch inputs and terminal token reserve. One threshold-crossing buy secures `Q=threshold+2 raw`; ten valid preceding buy/sell round trips secure `Q=threshold+22 raw`. The [minimized repeatable-pump and liveness evidence](../../../evidence/launchpad/kuru/completion-liveness-research-2026-09-30.md) reduces the buy to two raw quote units and verifies 16 repeats in Solidity and 1,000 in the exact model. The Curve's real quote reserve rises by one raw unit per round trip even though its token reserve returns to its initial value. Neither witness proves a global reachable-`Q` maximum. Consequently the existing profile matrix at `Q=threshold` is a scenario, not a safe pre-bonding Kuru-admission predicate.

| Option | Consequence | Current assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prove every reachable terminal `Q` stays in a finite supported envelope under the current buy/sell, fee sweep and buyback semantics; then fork-check its boundaries | No change to Core economics if the proof succeeds; an accepted Kuru-bound launch remains seedable | Deprioritized: a minimal cycle repeatedly pumps real quote and no useful configuration-derived ceiling is currently proven. Do not keep accumulating one-shot profiles as a substitute. |
| Accept an immutable **generic** `graduationQuoteCeiling` at launch; the venue policy derives its value and the Curve requires exact `completionTerminalQuote(state) <= ceiling` after every accepted economic transition | Enforces an arithmetic immediate-completion path without a `venue == KURU` branch in the Curve; may reject trades/sweeps that currently succeed, so economic semantics and existing-launch migration require owner approval | Recommended research direction. Exact Python and native/Circle Solidity differential fixtures pass for sampled states, but a production guard, full transition inventory/stateful proof and accepted ceiling are still absent. |
| Check actual `Q` only after the launch reaches `GRADUATING` | Could leave an otherwise valid bonded launch permanently unable to graduate under P0's no-rescue/no-fallback rule | **Reject as sole admission rule**; retain the runtime check as defense in depth |
| Adapt the Kuru tuple at phase 2 | Changes the predicted CREATE2 destination and potentially market economics; a finite menu still needs universal coverage of reachable `Q` | Not a substitute for prelaunch proof without a separately accepted deterministic policy |

Before ADR-021 can be accepted, choose the supported launch/configuration envelope, accepted seed/tick error, min-size anti-spam policy and an immutable generic completion ceiling. Re-run exact Python and real pinned-fork boundary cases, including fee sweeps, buyback and round trips, then prove the guard preserves a completion path after every accepted transition. The current Factory's one-token supply floor already conflicts with the candidate Kuru minimum size. This is a genuine policy and architecture dependency, not a test-count deficit.
