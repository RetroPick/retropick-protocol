# Growth plan

RetroPick intends to earn a leading position through market quality, distribution and retained users. It does not claim leadership today.

| Wedge | Initial offer | Validation and guardrails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monad-native degen launches | Simple MON issuance → bonding → Kuru flow | Measure onboarding, completion and retained trading; distinguish tests from adoption |
| Creator / community launches | Reliable primary discovery and protected graduation assets | Recruit documented design partners; distribution/revenue-sharing features only after qualification |
| Embedded launch infrastructure | SDK/API/widgets for wallets, communities and apps | Partner discovery first, then reproducible integration and support metrics |
| Verified new asset classes | Additional qualified quote assets and issuer-specific assets | Exact chain/address, transfer behavior, issuer restrictions, venue/economic/liveness testing; no xStocks/PreStocks working claim |

Initial channels: [RetroPick X](https://x.com/RetroPickMarket), existing Telegram/Discord communities, Monad creator cohorts and developer integrations. Founder-reported waitlist and engagement are distribution signals pending evidence, not paid customers or verified market demand. No interviews or partner commitments are invented.

Pilot: recruit a small creator/community cohort, observe wallet connection and launch friction, record launch-to-graduation conversion, execution spreads/depth and return activity, and obtain consented feedback. Exclude internal test traffic and define every metric/time window before reporting results.

Potential moat: market-origination reliability + Kuru integration + launch history/reputation + distribution + developer integrations. A permanent liquidity lock is a narrow custody property; it does not prevent scams or guarantee price stability. Reputation, transparent launch policy and education should complement it.

Scale after evidence: prioritize creator retention and secondary market quality over raw launch count. Seek wallet/community design partners and market-maker participation without implying agreements already exist. [30/60/90 plan and partnership ask](MONAD_PARTNERSHIP.md).
