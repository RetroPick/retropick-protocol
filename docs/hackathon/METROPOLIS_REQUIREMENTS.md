## Deadline and competition

Submission closes **13 October 2026, 11:59 PM ET**, equivalent to **14 October, 03:59 UTC / 10:59 Bangkok**. Submit early; the recorded portal version and timestamp control. The rules and portal agree on the deadline. Rules list judging 14–27 October and winners 3 November; the dashboard lists judging through 3 November and winners from 4 November. Confirm the later schedule with organizers rather than treating this discrepancy as resolved.

Four primary tracks have $30,000 each, split across three $10,000 awards. Grand Champion is $25,000. The public $250K+ figure includes sponsor prizes/tooling; the rules' main prize pool is $145,000. Continued ecosystem support/residency is an opportunity, not an entitlement or partnership commitment.

Primary track: **Onchain Finance & Trading**. The portal describes asset primitives, market structures and usable trading experiences enabled by fast, cheap settlement. Rules focus on financial instruments, markets and asset primitives for traders, protocols and financial-product builders.

## Eligibility and repository obligations

- One project per participant; exactly one primary track. Team size: 1–5, with designated primary contact.
- Participants must meet age, geography, sanctions and organizer/sponsor conflict rules. Age is 18+ as of 1 September; younger participation requires verifiable guardian consent. Team must attest to its own eligibility.
- Majority of work must be during the build window. Identify pre-existing foundations and substantial new functionality honestly.
- Working Monad mainnet/testnet prototype, reproducible code, deployed addresses and transaction evidence.
- Public GitHub source, OSI open-source license, README setup, attribution and build-window commit history. The form permits shared GitHub access, but public source satisfies the stricter rules.
- AI tools are permitted and **must be disclosed in README**.
- No fake volume, wash trading or misleading claims. Our two-wallet test transactions are functional testnet evidence, not organic demand.
