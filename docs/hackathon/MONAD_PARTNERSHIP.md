# RetroPick × Monad × Kuru

We want to become the highest-quality market-origination layer on Monad and prove it through launch volume, market quality and retained traders.

Monad provides high-performance settlement. Kuru provides mature secondary trading infrastructure. RetroPick provides asset issuance, primary price discovery and market origination. Together, creator/community → RetroPick → Monad settlement → Kuru → secondary markets.

## Current proof

The V2 testnet candidate issued a token, traded on its bonding curve, secured assets through one authoritative Coordinator, created a real Kuru market and vault, permanently protected LP/excess assets, and completed a two-wallet order/fill/cancel test. [Evidence](EVIDENCE_MAP.md).

Historical release: 145 tests passed, zero failed/skipped; Factory runtime 23,423 bytes. Canonical Circle-USDC live smoke is BLOCKED_FUNDING. These are engineering results, not usage metrics, revenue or commercial validation.

The founder reports 30+ waitlist signups and approximately 300K X engagements. Analytics verification is pending. Communities provide a potential discovery channel; no confirmed design partners, pilots or market-maker agreements exist. [Claim boundaries](DEMAND_EVIDENCE.md).

## Future value and measurement

| KPI | What we will measure | Present evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launches / unique creators | Valid Factory launches and distinct initiating wallets, excluding tests | No adoption claim |
| Unique traders / bonding volume | Distinct traders and quote flows, separating test traffic | Functional test proof only |
| Graduation rate / liquidity | Completed launches divided by eligible launches; actual seeded quote and protected shares | One canonical test lifecycle |
| RetroPick-originated Kuru markets | Coordinator receipts with verified destination identities | Canonical demo verified |
| Post-graduation Kuru volume | Executed trades, attributed without double counting | No organic volume claim |
| Creator / trader retention | Cohort return activity over defined periods | Not measured |
| Partner-originated launches | Documented source attribution and qualified launch events | No confirmed partners |

Targets will be set after a small pilot baseline, rather than inventing current traction or arbitrary volume promises. More qualified launches could generate primary trading, graduation transactions, new Kuru markets, secondary activity and market-maker opportunities. Distribution and quality must be validated together.

## Concrete ask

We seek technical and ecosystem mentorship; eligible Metropolis residency/builder support; introductions to Monad wallets, communities and asset issuers; coordination with Kuru on qualified production integration; available RPC/indexing infrastructure support; market-maker/liquidity introductions; co-marketing for qualified launches; security/audit introductions; and guidance on earning a canonical market-origination role through measured quality.

RetroPick would contribute creators, launches, distribution, primary discovery and graduation liquidity. Monad contributes settlement and ecosystem channels. Kuru contributes orderbook infrastructure, trader access and market-making workflows. No exclusive or official partnership is claimed.

## Business model and company case

Potential revenue surfaces include launch, bonding and graduation fees; policy-permitted post-launch protocol/creator fees; B2B launch infrastructure; and partner integrations. These are opportunities to validate, not invented revenue figures or a claim that every fee exists today.

Creators → launches → traders → graduated markets → Kuru liquidity/activity → distribution → more creators is the proposed flywheel. Long-term interfaces could include a consumer app, SDK, API and embedded launch widget for communities, wallets, apps, games and agents. Tokenized asset issuers are a future research/customer category requiring exact-contract, transfer, issuer/legal and economic qualification. They are not implemented integrations.
