## 30 seconds

“Monad provides high-performance settlement, and Kuru provides secondary trading infrastructure. Every new asset still faces a cold-start market problem. RetroPick connects issuance and bonding-curve price discovery to a real Kuru market, with permanently protected graduation liquidity. Our Monad Testnet demo has launched, graduated and completed a two-wallet order, fill and cancel. We are building the market creation layer for Monad.”

## 60 seconds

“Launching a token is easy. Creating a credible market is harder. Creators need primary discovery, initial liquidity and a usable secondary venue.

RetroPick connects those stages on Monad. A creator launches through transparent onchain bonding. The venue and policy are fixed before trading. When bonding completes, our Coordinator secures the assets and a Kuru executor creates and seeds the market. Graduation LP and excess tokens enter permanent custody.

This is real testnet execution: issuance, bonding, Kuru graduation and a two-wallet order, fill and cancel. The historical candidate passed 145 tests, with no failures or skips. It remains unaudited and testnet-only.

Our next step is a creator pilot, market-quality measurement and independent security review. We want to earn a role as Monad's market-origination layer through quality and retained traders.”

## Two minutes — include team introduction

“I’m [founder name], building RetroPick with [accurate team introduction]. We are building the market creation layer for Monad.

New communities can issue tokens quickly, but issuance does not create a mature market. They need price discovery, initial liquidity and a place where traders can use explicit prices and market-making workflows.

RetroPick provides a two-stage lifecycle. A token starts on an onchain bonding curve. The creator commits its MON quote policy and Kuru destination before trading. Once the sellable allocation is exhausted, a dedicated Coordinator secures the assets. A venue executor verifies Kuru, creates the market, deposits initial liquidity and permanently protects the graduation LP and excess tokens. If the venue transaction fails, secured assets remain available for permissionless retry.

We are not rebuilding an exchange for every token. RetroPick specializes in origination; Kuru provides secondary execution. Monad settles both phases with the execution model needed for frequent transactions and many simultaneous markets.

The canonical demo is live on Monad Testnet. It issued a token, completed bonding, created a real Kuru market and vault, and performed a two-wallet order, fill and cancel. These are functional tests, not organic adoption. The historical candidate passed 145 tests and kept the Factory below EIP-170 through architectural extraction. It is unaudited and not mainnet-ready.

We have early founder-reported audience interest, which we are verifying. Next comes creator discovery, pilot measurement, testnet hardening and external security review. We seek Monad ecosystem mentorship, qualified partner introductions and coordination with Kuru. Our ambition is to earn a canonical role through credible markets, launch quality and retained traders.”
