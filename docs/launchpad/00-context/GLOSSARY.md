# Glossary

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Owner:** Protocol

- **Launch:** one token plus its configured primary-market lifecycle.
- **Launch inventory:** token supply committed to primary distribution.
- **Quote asset:** asset used to buy/sell and denominate launch economics.
- **Real quote reserve:** actual quote assets held by the primary mechanism.
- **Virtual/phantom reserve:** accounting reserve used by the price function but not necessarily withdrawable collateral.
- **Bonding curve:** deterministic primary-market price/distribution mechanism.
- **Graduation threshold:** condition that makes a launch eligible to leave the primary market.
- **Graduation:** transition from launch-specific price discovery to mature secondary trading.
- **Same-quote graduation:** destination market uses the same quote denomination as the primary market.
- **Kuru market:** target mature secondary market for V2 on Monad.
- **V1:** stable/reference RetroPick launchpad generation.
- **V2:** active RetroPick launchpad generation.
