# Competitor Matrix

**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Product + Research  
**Authority:** Research input

## Purpose

Compare modern launchpads by product and market structure rather than by branding.

## Requirements

- Compare launch asset type, supply model, primary price discovery, quote assets, fees, graduation, mature venue, liquidity model, creator economics and extensibility.
- Cover representative systems such as Pump-style bonding launchpads, Clanker-style configurable token deployment, direct-market launchpads, Meteora/Raydium-style migration systems and other current Monad/EVM launchpads.
- Use findings to identify RetroPick differentiation: structured primary launch plus mature Kuru trading.

## Non-goals

- Do not rank competitors without explicit criteria.
- Do not treat copied implementation details as RetroPick requirements.

## Acceptance criteria

- Matrix exposes at least one trade-off for every compared mechanism.

## Evidence required

- Dated primary sources where available.
