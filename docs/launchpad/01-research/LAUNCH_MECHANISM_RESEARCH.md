# Launch Mechanism Research

**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Protocol + Research  
**Authority:** Research input

## Purpose

Evaluate primary-market mechanisms that can distribute a new token before mature secondary trading.

## Requirements

- Compare constant-product bonding curves, fixed-price sale, batch auction, direct-market launch and liquidity-bootstrap mechanisms.
- Evaluate capital efficiency, manipulation surface, price continuity, creator UX, launch duration, deterministic graduation and implementability on Monad.
- Treat the existing bonding-curve mechanism as the P0 implementation baseline; alternatives are future options unless accepted by ADR.

## Non-goals

- No Prediction/PRISM mechanisms.
- No mechanism change without LP-MATH-1 and LP-ARCH-1.

## Acceptance criteria

- P0 recommendation has explicit economic/security rationale.

## Evidence required

- References, model calculations and adversarial scenarios.
