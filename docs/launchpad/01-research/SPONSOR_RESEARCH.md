# Sponsor Research

**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Hackathon + Research  
**Authority:** Research input

## Purpose

Track Metropolis sponsor capabilities and bounty requirements that genuinely fit the launchpad.

## Requirements

- Verify current bounty rules close to submission.
- Map each candidate integration to a real launchpad user or engineering benefit.
- Classify P0/P1/P2 and define the evidence required for sponsor credit.

## Non-goals

- Do not distort core architecture solely to collect bounties.
- Do not rely on stale event notes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every sponsor integration has a product rationale, cut priority and proof plan.

## Evidence required

- Current hackathon pages and sponsor technical docs.
