# Launchpad Invariants

**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Protocol + Security  
**Authority:** Canonical protocol specification

## Purpose

Own safety properties independent of UI.

## Requirements

- Supply conservation.
- Real reserve and fee conservation.
- No unauthorized mint or reserve seizure.
- No double graduation or lost graduation assets.
- Fee caps hold.
- Post-graduation primary state is terminal.
- Quote policy cannot be bypassed.

## Non-goals

- Does not redefine Prediction/PRISM.

## Acceptance criteria

- Requirements are testable and consistent with PROTOCOL_SPEC.

## Evidence required

- Requirement-to-test traceability.
