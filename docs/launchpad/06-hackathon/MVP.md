# Hackathon MVP

**Status:** P0  
**Owner:** Hackathon Technical Lead

## Golden path

1. Connect wallet.
2. Browse launches.
3. Open Create.
4. Enter token metadata, quote asset, supply and supported creator settings.
5. Review economics.
6. Sign launch creation.
7. Launch becomes ACTIVE and appears in discovery.
8. Trader buys; price/progress/fees update.
9. Trader sells; balances/reserves/activity reconcile.
10. Additional demand crosses the graduation threshold.
11. Launch becomes GRADUATION_READY.
12. Graduation secures assets and creates/identifies the Kuru market.
13. Kuru market is verified usable.
14. Launch page transitions to mature-market trading.
15. Evidence links expose creation/trade/graduation transactions and Kuru market identity.

## Done

No manual contract calls are required for the primary demo, and every onchain claim has receipt/state evidence.
