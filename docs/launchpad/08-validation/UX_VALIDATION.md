# UX Validation

**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Product + Validation  
**Authority:** Product quality gate

## Purpose

Validate that creators/traders understand price, fees, risks, progress and graduation.

## Requirements

- Test create review, buy/sell quote comprehension, slippage/fees, transaction progress, errors and post-graduation transition.
- Check accessibility, responsive behavior and stale-data indicators.
- Do not hide protocol risk behind celebratory launch UI.

## Non-goals

- Does not waive higher-authority safety requirements.

## Acceptance criteria

- Critical actions are understandable without developer explanation.

## Evidence required

- Recorded UX review and browser checks.
