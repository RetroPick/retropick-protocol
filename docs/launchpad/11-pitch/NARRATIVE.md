# Narrative

New tokens face two different problems: bootstrapping early distribution and supporting a mature market after demand appears. RetroPick treats them as two stages instead of forcing one venue to do both.

Creators launch fixed/capped ERC-20 assets into a transparent bonding primary market. Traders can see price, fees and launch progress onchain. Once the configured demand/liquidity threshold is reached, RetroPick safely transitions the asset into Kuru, where continuous secondary trading can take over.

The hackathon proof is the full lifecycle, not a static token generator: create, trade, graduate and keep trading.
