# Trading history changes the terminal Kuru seed inputs

**Classification:** `COUNTEREXAMPLE_FOUND` for threshold-only launch admission; bounded path witness, **not** a proof that terminal `Q` is unbounded. **Chain:** Monad Testnet 10143. **Fork:** block 66752717 for canonical Circle test USDC. No broadcast or signer was used.

The prior [one-shot witness](prelaunch-q-threshold-counterexample-2026-09-30.md) establishes `Q = threshold + 2 raw units` for a launch with one million initial tokens, phantom quote and threshold both 100 quote units, a 100-bps curve fee, 50-bps creator tax, and no buyback. The new path runs ten valid `buy(10 raw quote) → sell(exactly the purchased tokens)` round trips before the same threshold-crossing buy. The ten-raw-unit trades have zero individually floored fee and tax, so this path isolates integer reserve rounding rather than a changed fee policy.

After each round trip, the launch-token reserve returns to its original one-million-token balance, while tradeable real quote grows by **one raw unit**. After ten rounds, the terminal crossing therefore starts at `R=10`, and the secured quote becomes `threshold + 22`, not `threshold + 2`. The terminal token reserve remains 500,000 tokens in both paths, but `B=floor(TQ/(P+Q))` changes.

The [exact machine fixture](prelaunch-round-trip-witness-2026-09-30.json) is generated by `python3 research/integration/kuru/prelaunch_round_trip_witness.py`. The Python model independently matches real current Curve/Factory transitions asserted in [the native/Circle Foundry tests](../../../contracts/test/v2/integration/RetroPickV4GraduationBehavior.t.sol). The V4 destination seam is synthetic; the Curve, Factory, transfers and Circle token on the pinned fork are real.

Reproduction: `python3 -m unittest discover -s research/integration/kuru -p 'test_*.py' -v` passed **18/18**. With `MONAD_TESTNET_RPC_URL` loaded from the existing `contracts/.env.local`, `forge test --match-path test/v2/integration/RetroPickV4GraduationBehavior.t.sol --summary` passed **4/4**, zero skips. Toolchain: Forge 1.8.3, solc 0.8.26, optimizer 200, viaIR under the current local Foundry configuration.

**Qualification consequence:** a profile evaluated only at `Q=threshold`, or even at the one-shot `threshold+2`, cannot prove every Kuru-bound launch will be seedable. This bounded path does **not** establish a maximum `Q`, a profitable attack, or a direct insolvency. The next admissibility proof must encompass every reachable trading history under a frozen Core fee/buyback policy, or an accepted Core policy must constrain the future reserve domain. A phase-2 measured-`Q` check remains necessary but cannot retroactively reject an unsafe launch before public bonding. `KURU-B3`, `KURU-B4`, and `KURU_TARGET_DEV_FROZEN` remain unqualified.
