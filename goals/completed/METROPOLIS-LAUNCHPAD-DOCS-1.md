## Objective

Establish a native RetroPick documentation lane for the Monad launchpad covering product, protocol, smart contracts, full-stack architecture, integrations, hackathon execution, validation, security, production operations, evidence and pitch.

## Acceptance

- `docs/launchpad/` routes the launchpad source of truth.
- `contracts/docs/launchpad/` owns Solidity implementation documentation.
- V1/V2 development policy is explicit.
- Prediction/PRISM requirements remain separate.
- obsolete active-tree rename/migration documentation is absent.
- required legal/dependency notices remain under contract legal files.
- launchpad phase gates and evidence model are defined.

Validation evidence is recorded under `evidence/launchpad/`.
