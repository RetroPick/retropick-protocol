# Executive decision

**Decision: continue research; no promotion.** This checkpoint freezes the source baseline and provides executable protection checks. It does not fulfill the complete experiment acceptance gates.

The intended model preserves protocol fees and makes the remaining base-fee share plus creator tax programmable. This is an engineering default delegated by the user after requesting deeper Pump.fun analysis; representative demand and optimal fee levels remain unproven. The user explicitly selected launch-precommitted recovery authority.

Current source already has fixed initial issuance, exact-transfer pull escrow, five-year buyback vesting, coordinator-led graduation, Kuru and v4 executors, and permanent liquidity custody. New economic guarantees cannot be achieved by simply adding a policy registry: existing recipient override, fee rescue and vest-beneficiary mutation paths must be constrained in the opt-in experimental mode.

Continuous Kuru creator/community rewards are BLOCKED_EXTERNAL_CAPABILITY. Source-level capability evidence does not establish a deployed right to Kuru protocol fees or a harvest path from protected LP shares. Do not market the experiment as ongoing Kuru-funded yield.

Canonical v1/v2, existing tests, manifests and production consumers remain read-only. No deployment, ABI consumer migration, V3, proxy upgrade or accepted ADR modification is authorized.
