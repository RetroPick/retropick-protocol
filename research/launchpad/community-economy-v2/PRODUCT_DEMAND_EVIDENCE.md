# Product demand evidence — scoped update

Reviewed 2026-10-31. The supplied report's opaque citation IDs are not evidence. The following original sources replace several core premises; none is a representative survey of RetroPick users.

| Evidence | Classification | Decision supported / limits |
|---|---|---|
| [Pump official fee schedule](https://pump.fun/docs/fees), updated 20 May 2026 | PRIMARY PLATFORM DOCUMENTATION | Distinguishes creator, protocol and LP fees; creator monetization is already competitive functionality. No retention or RetroPick conversion evidence. |
| [Pump official fee-sharing instructions](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/main/docs/instructions/CREATOR_FEE_SHARING.md) | PRIMARY TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION | Describes multi-recipient distribution and sweeping pending AMM/curve fees before its one-time final share update; supports the need to preserve existing entitlements. | [Szwajcok et al., Meme Coin Factories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10246v1), submitted 9 September 2026 | INDEPENDENT RESEARCH, ABSTRACT REVIEWED | Authors report automated manipulation including wash trading and creator obfuscation. Supports adversarial modeling; data/methods were not independently reproduced here and do not establish demand for rewards. |

Observed job: verifiable fee beneficiaries and reliable stage-specific collection. Hypothesis: creators/contributors value recurring, enforceable splits enough to choose RetroPick. Current experiment directly tests enforcement, not that commercial hypothesis. Trader priorities of fair entry, slippage and exit remain product requirements; no new market survey quantifies them. Mainstream clarity and treasury continuity require user testing and qualified custody modules.

Trading-fee allocation is the only implemented income source. External-business revenue rights, profit promises, passive-holder earnings and guaranteed APY are excluded. Stake-to-earn would apply only to qualifying stakers, not every holder. Buybacks are spending strategies, not a guarantee of price appreciation.

Next evidence dependency: interviews with creators/contributors/traders, representative sampling design and measurable launch/exit/claim friction. Track willingness to use bounded future allocation versus a frozen split, after gas and fee disclosure; do not treat gross volume as retention or genuine adoption.
