## Review conclusion

No new asset-theft exploit was demonstrated by this review of the baseline community custody/control modules. That observation is bounded: historical Slither triage is contextual source analysis, and new M6 venue code is not approved by analogy to old Curve-only code. Optimized compiler applicability, new source authentication, exact multi-asset funding, deployment binding, epoch/callback safety, genuine-v4 and actual-Safe verification remain explicit qualification gates.

The previous timelock restrictions are enforced by the qualified subclass, not by the upstream primitive alone: fixed72-hour delay; permanently open executor; self-admin; nonzero governed proposer/canceller changes; disabled role renunciation; seven-day execution window inclusive at the boundary. Losing the last proposer is an admitted governance liveness risk. Controller recovery grants no treasury spend authority. A signer may already control both roles in real life; contracts cannot establish independence of beneficial ownership.
