## Source-confirmed safeguards and their composition

1. **Old-policy checkpoint is substantive.** Canonical Curve `communityPending()` includes fee/tax/buyback buckets and a settlement sentinel (`RetroPickBondingCurveV2.sol:114–115`). Its checkpoint always enters `nonReentrant`, even when apparent buckets are zero (118–124). Settlement zeros buckets and removes exactly protocol+community value from tracked custody before authenticated Router funding (126–150). Registry calls this before epoch increment. A malicious callback cannot be dismissed merely because a bucket was zero; the source guard is essential.
2. **No second protocol cut at the Curve Router boundary.** Curve computes `P=f*share/10000`, `C=f-P+t` at 133–134. Canonical Router allocates the received C without recomputing P (106–125). M6 venue boundary intentionally receives gross and performs its own one-time P calculation (Router 310–319). These are different boundaries, not interchangeable APIs.
3. **Donation is not an allocation operation.** Plain receives do not mutate funded/credits. Funding checks old backing first; unsolicited surplus does not authorize an arbitrary fee-source caller. Treasury earnings counters move only after its own Router credit was consumed and actual receipt reconciled.
4. **Claims are address-owned, not controller-owned.** Canonical Router decrements `credit[msg.sender]`; M6 decrements `credits[asset][msg.sender]`. Controller rotation updates no such entry. No reviewed Controller function can call Vault spending with timelock authority.
5. **Bounding active work avoids epoch-history loops.** Allocation cardinality is at most ten; claims update a single beneficiary. Policy history grows, but claim/fund paths do not iterate all previous epochs.
6. **The 72-hour delay is actually restricted.** `updateDelay` always reverts and executor/admin membership cannot be changed by `grantRole/revokeRole` overrides. A timelocked arbitrary target call is not an immediate owner bypass. Actual Safe signatures and operational proposer retention remain separate requirements.

These observations are source evidence, not assertions that every adversarial sequence has passed tests.
