# PRISM Kill Criteria

Stop or redesign the affected mechanism if evidence shows:
- unbacked mint is possible;
- backing can be double allocated;
- valid redemption can underback remaining supply;
- fixed-point rounding permits positive-value extraction;
- final redemption can become underfunded;
- payoff admission accepts a non-replicable claim as exact;
- lifecycle permits unsafe resurrection or duplicate finalization;
- a required external dependency would become authoritative for same-chain backing;
- product value depends on market behavior that repeatedly fails under realistic evidence.

A PRISM kill criterion does not block Launchpad Core.
