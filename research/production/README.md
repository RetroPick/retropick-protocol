# RetroPick Production Research OS

**Scope:** shared RetroPick platform infrastructure plus Launchpad Core.

PRISM consumes shared-platform research where relevant but remains outside the normal production track until module admission.

## Purpose

Make production decisions current-source verified, version-pinned, benchmarked, reproducible, evidence-backed and explicit about uncertainty.

Research does not silently redefine canonical product/protocol semantics.

## Structure

- control/: machine-readable research state and gates
- schemas/: source/claim/environment/benchmark schemas
- platform/: shared infrastructure research
- launchpad-core/: Launchpad-specific production research
- benchmarks/: benchmark methodology/specifications
- experiments/: reproducible experiment manifests
- matrices/: gaps, dependencies, security, risks, module promotion
- reports/: current research conclusions
- tooling/: deterministic validators/aggregators
