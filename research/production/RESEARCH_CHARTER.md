# Production Research Charter

Primary targets:
1. RetroPick shared platform infrastructure.
2. Launchpad Core production engineering.

PRISM may reuse shared research for Monad, Kuru, RPC, wallets, indexing, APIs, observability and CI/CD, but remains under its own mechanism and module-admission gates.

Production Research does not own protocol economics, PRISM mathematics, collateral rules, lifecycle invariants, admin authorization or deployment authorization.

When evidence contradicts canonical assumptions, stop the affected path and propose an ADR/doc change with evidence.
