# Source Policy

Tier 1: official docs, repositories, deployment registries, SDK/API docs and canonical standards.

Tier 2: official engineering/release notes, maintainer issues/PRs and official announcements.

Tier 3: community examples, third-party tutorials and social/community discussion.

Tier 3 may generate hypotheses but cannot define production architecture.

Mutable facts such as addresses, endpoints, APIs, chain IDs, rate limits, Kuru interfaces and network parameters require retrieval date, version/commit when available, source URL, uncertainty and a reverification trigger.
